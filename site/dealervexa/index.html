FAQ

Questions dealers ask.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Buy it anywhere (GoDaddy, Namecheap…), tell us, and we connect it for you. Until then your site is live on your DealerVexa link.

How do my cars get to CARFAX, Cars.com and Facebook?

Each site gives you a dealer ID. Put it in Marketing and send your inventory as a file, a feed link, or automatically by FTP every day. For Craigslist and Marketplace we write the ad for you.

Does the AI photo studio cost extra?

No. It runs on your computer, so there are no per-photo fees.

Can I run buy-here-pay-here?

Yes: schedules, payments, receipts, payoff and late accounts are built in.

Can I add CARFAX badges, Google Analytics or a Facebook Pixel?

Yes. Paste their code in Settings → Custom code and it goes on your website.

What if I get stuck?

Press Help in your panel. Our team answers there and can open your panel to fix things with you.
