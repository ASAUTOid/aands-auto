Our story

Rooted in Burley.
Built on trust.

A & S Auto started with a simple idea: people in Mini-Cassia deserve big-city selection with small-town honesty.

From our home at 1038 Overland Ave, we hand-select every vehicle on our lot, from work-ready trucks that can handle an Idaho winter to family SUVs and weekend cars you'd normally drive hours to find.

Every car goes through a 150-point inspection and a full detail, and comes with a transparent vehicle history report. No hidden fees, no pressure. Just straight answers and fair prices.

Our customers are our neighbours. That's why so many of them come back, and why they send their friends and family our way.
